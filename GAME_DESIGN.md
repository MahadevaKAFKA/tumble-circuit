# Game design — Tumble Circuit: Chaos Drop (as built, Phases A–D)

**Pitch:** a fast, fair marble race for 1–16 racers on Roblox; bots fill the grid. Every marble
rolls the same; skill, lines and abilities decide races; everything you earn is cosmetic.

## Loop
Hub (Orbital Garage) → intermission 15 s (map vote in the first 8 s with 2+ people; READY can
start a full race early) → frozen grid → 3-2-1 → race (≤ 180 s) → results 12 s → hub.
Modes: Chaos Race (default), **Time Trial** (solo, PB ghost), **Party Race** (Roblox party
members start side by side, max 4).

## Worlds (3, rotating)
Neon Foundry (16 modules), Sky Carnival (14), Volcano Vault (14). 8 server checkpoints each,
3 hazard variations per world, Chaos Gates, trick ramp, grapple rings. Hazards: bumpers,
sweepers/rings, hammers/pistons/flippers, fans/wind/geysers, trampolines/drums, falling tiles,
conveyors; rockfall, lava and cloud visuals.

## Movement & skill
Along-track accel 35, lateral 22, terminal 80, brake, 40% air control; Ramp Trick, Slipstream,
Near Miss, Perfect Dodge, cooldown charge. 12 abilities, two slots: Boost, Shield, Pulse
(starters) + Frost Wake, Magnet, Phase, Grapple Spark, Bounce Burst, Gravity Glide, Reverse Jet,
Decoy, Anchor (level 3/6/10 or earned Coins).

## Progression (all earned)
Coins & XP per race (bot-practice limits), levels 1–50 with a cosmetic every 5 levels,
3 daily + 4 weekly quests, free 28-day season (30 levels), free capsules (600 Coins, published
odds and pity), Shards from duplicates → Shard Shop. Leaderboards: best time per world and
variation, weekly wins (2+ humans).

## Cosmetics
35 marbles, 12 trails, 8 finish bursts, 8 preset emotes (+ 6 clearly-marked optional extras for
the off-by-default shop). Visual only.

## Not in the game
Paid random items, trading, chat beyond Roblox's, free-text emotes, combat bonuses for money.
