# Creator capture guide (v40)

How to record clean clips of Tumble Circuit on a phone. The game never records, uploads or
posts anything by itself; you record with your device and decide what to share.

## Quick setup (Samsung Galaxy, e.g. S25 Ultra)

1. Swipe down twice → **Screen recorder**. Sound: *Media sounds* (no microphone unless you
   want commentary). Video quality: 1080p is plenty.
2. In the game: **Settings → Graphics** to the highest level the phone holds at a steady frame
   rate (Auto counts as Low on touch devices; pick a level by hand for recording).
3. Hold the phone in landscape. Roblox runs landscape; vertical clips are cropped afterwards.

## Clean Capture (challenges)

1. **MORE → CHALLENGE → CLEAN CAPTURE: ON**, then **TRY**.
2. While you drive, the challenge buttons and the top HUD hide; a small timer stays; the
   touch stick and brake stay. RETRY, QUIT and the result card come back when the attempt ends.
3. A **9:16 guide** shades the sides of the screen. Keep the marble inside the clear middle
   band and a vertical crop (Shorts, TikTok, Reels) will not lose it.
4. Turn it off on the same card when you are done.

## Good shots

- **The Drop Shaft precision entry:** take the right kicker at the end of the brake zone and
  land past the QUALIFYING LINE sign – the result card says SIGNATURE LINE when you do.
- **Ghost duels:** after a PB, RETRY and race your own mint ghost; with a friend in the same
  server, both share their ghost (SHARE MY GHOST) for a two-ghost race.
- **Race gaps:** in normal races the gate gap ("+0.84 to MIA") and the RIVAL tag make overtakes
  readable on screen.

## Respect other players

- Other players' names show on nameplates and the gap chip. Ask before posting a clip where
  someone else is clearly identifiable, and never post anyone's chat.
- Do not post clips that show private-server links or codes you don't want public.
