# Promotional concept art (owner-provided)

These four images came with the owner's Promo and Builder Pack (2026-11-26). They are
AI-generated promotional illustrations, **not screenshots of the game**:
- 00: experience icon, 512 × 512;
- 01–03: thumbnails, 1920 × 1080.

Uploading them is the owner's action in Creator Dashboard → the experience → Places/Thumbnails
and Icon. Roblox expects thumbnails to represent the experience. Prompt 24 replaces them with
real captures once the matching scenes exist in the game.

They live here, outside `src/`, so they never enter the place file.
