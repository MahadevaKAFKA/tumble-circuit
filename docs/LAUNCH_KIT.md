**Title:** Tumble Circuit: Chaos Drop

**Description:**
Roll, steer and dodge through three marble race worlds — the Neon Foundry, Sky Carnival and
Volcano Vault. Up to 16 racers (bots fill empty spots) race through bumpers, sweepers, hammers,
geysers, wind lanes and falling tiles, with 8 checkpoints so a slip never ends your race.

- Time Trial with your personal-best ghost; race with your Roblox party side by side.
- 35 marble looks, trails, finish bursts and emotes to earn — every marble rolls the same.
- Daily and weekly quests, a free 30-level season, free capsules with the odds shown up front.
- Phone, PC and controller.
